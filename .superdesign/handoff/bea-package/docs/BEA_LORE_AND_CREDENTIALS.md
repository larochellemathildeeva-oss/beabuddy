# Béa Lore & Questionable Credentials

## Recommendation

Keep Béa's fake academic history as its **own lore system**, not inside the personality sliders.

Why:

- personality answers **how Béa speaks**
- quirks answer **what dog-like habits Béa has**
- lore answers **what ridiculous backstory Béa believes about herself**

That separation keeps the system maintainable and lets the same recurring joke adapt to different personalities.

## Architecture

Use:

`context → personality trait → optional quirk → optional lore reference → final line`

Lore should be rare.

Recommended frequency: **5–12% of eligible Béa comments**.

## Primary institution

The most recognizable recurring school should be:

**The Canine Institute of Travel**

This becomes the closest thing Béa has to a canonical alma mater.

Secondary institutions can occasionally appear because Béa's academic history is intentionally unreliable:

- Academy of Travel & Treat Sciences
- Canine College of Cartography
- Institute of Advanced Sniffing & Navigation
- Academy of Tiny-Leg Logistics
- International Institute of Very Serious Dogs

The contradictions are part of the joke.

## Optional Profile Easter egg

Under:

**Profile → Béa**

add a tiny non-settings card:

**Béa's credentials**

This is optional but recommended because it rewards users who explore the profile.

It can rotate one fake credential such as:

- Canine Institute of Travel — Travel Planning
- Academy of Travel & Treat Sciences — Minor in Snacks
- Institute of Advanced Sniffing & Navigation — Honors
- Academy of Tiny-Leg Logistics — Continuing Education

It should feel like an Easter egg, not a feature users have to configure.

## Important

Never imply that any fictional school is a real accredited institution.

Suppress lore completely for payments, account/security, safety issues, destructive actions, serious errors, or other contexts where clarity matters more than character.
