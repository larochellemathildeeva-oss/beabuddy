Definition of done
Béa has eight user-adjustable personality dimensions: Helpful, Funny, Sassy, Encouraging, Curious, Adventurous, Dramatic and Chill. The user may adjust the percentages freely; normalize the resulting mix to 100%. The landing You page shows only the current overall mode. Tapping Béa opens the detailed controls. Mode naming is dynamically derived from the selected combination, with supported presets and Custom behavior as specified later in this master.
Béa personality architecture
Do not design Trip Documents as storage for passport scans, visas, government IDs, payment-card numbers, banking credentials or passwords. It is for trip-specific bookings, confirmations, tickets, reservations, receipts/instructions and user-added trip files.
Trip booking/document information must be represented as one structured underlying record with multiple access points, never duplicated. A booking/document can be added from You → Trip documents, assigned to a trip, optionally linked to a specific itinerary event, shown in that trip’s overview/bookings area, and opened from the associated event. Editing or deleting the record must update every view immediately.
Core cross-app architecture
Do not redesign, reinterpret, simplify or modernize locked screens. Do not substitute screenshots between sections. Do not regenerate Béa. Use the established user-supplied Béa mascot assets only. Calm uses neutral cream/ivory/beige/taupe/charcoal with restrained burnt-orange accents and no colorful pastel category tiles. Colorful may use restrained pastel accents while preserving identical information architecture. Dark uses black/charcoal with warm neutral accents. Bottom navigation is Home · World · Trips · Recs · You.

Cross-app booking/document data contract
Access point 1 — You → Trip documents: global inbox/library + unassigned items.
Access point 2 — Trip Overview → Bookings/Essentials: all bookings for that trip.
Access point 3 — Timeline/Companion event: contextual booking state/details for that event.
Create or import from any access point; assignment synchronizes all three.
Support flights, hotels/stays, restaurants, activities/tours/tickets, rental cars and transport.
Original attachment may coexist with parsed structured fields.
Béa may parse and suggest associations, but user confirms trip/event assignment.
Sensitive identity/payment credentials are out of scope.
