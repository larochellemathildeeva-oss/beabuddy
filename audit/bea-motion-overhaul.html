Interface proposal · motion & cohesion

Making Béa feel
like one app

Béa currently behaves like a well-written website with a tab bar bolted underneath. This is a proposal to rebuild the connective tissue — how screens relate, how they move, and how a person knows where they are — so it reads as a single designed product rather than twenty-one pages.

Baseline: v8.4.32 keyframes0 route transitions 6 tabs · 21 routes

Diagnosis

Nothing in the app moves between screens. Every navigation is a cut.

I measured the motion layer: two keyframes in the entire product — one fade-up, one pin pop — and no route transition of any kind. Tapping a tab replaces the screen instantly. Opening a trip expands an accordion in place. Nothing tells you whether you went sideways, deeper, or nowhere.

That single absence explains most of what reads as unpolished. Premium interfaces are not more decorated than Béa — they are more continuous. Apple, Airbnb and Linear all spend their motion budget on one thing: making the next screen feel like it came from the thing you tapped.

01 — The model

Three planes, and every move has a reverse

Before any animation is written, the app needs a spatial grammar. Every destination sits on exactly one of three planes, and the plane determines how you get there and how you get back. This is what makes motion legible rather than decorative.

PLANE 0

Tabs — lateral

Home, Trips, Places, World, You. Cross-fade with a 6px directional drift. No depth, no push. You are moving along a shelf, not into anything.

PLANE 1

Detail — deeper

A trip, a saved place, a city. Grows from the card you tapped and shrinks back into it. Back is always the reverse of forward.

PLANE 2

Sheets — on top

To-dos, packing, the vault, Béa's planner. Rise from the bottom over a dimmed page. Temporary by definition; they never navigate anywhere.

The rule that makes this work: a screen may only ever appear on one plane. Today the trip to-do list is a sheet and the trip stops are inline — same importance, different physics, no reason. One plane per thing, decided once.

02 — Timing

Four durations, three curves, nothing else

The app currently has 17 duration declarations and no system. Premium feel comes from restraint here: a small vocabulary applied consistently reads as intentional, while seventeen hand-picked values read as noise even when each one is fine.

--t-tap120ms Colour, opacity, pressed states. Below perception — feels instant but not abrupt.
--t-shift200ms Chips, toggles, disclosure, the tab indicator settling.
--t-move320ms Sheets rising, tabs crossing, anything that changes what you are looking at.
--t-arrive420ms Card expanding into a detail page. The only duration allowed above 350ms.
--ease-standard cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1) Everything arriving. Already in the codebase — keep it, it is a good curve.
--ease-exit cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1) Anything leaving, at 60% of its entry duration. Exits should be faster than entrances.
--ease-confirm cubic-bezier(.34,1.4,.5,1) Reserved. A saved pin, a ticked to-do. Never for navigation — bounce on a page reads as cheap.

The tab bar should carry you, not cut

04 — Demonstration

A trip should open, not expand

Today tapping a trip unfolds an accordion, pushing every other trip off screen. It should become a page that grows out of the card you touched and shrinks back into it — the single highest-value motion change available, and the one users read as "expensive".

Implement with the View Transitions API and a shared view-transition-name on the trip photo; TanStack Router supports it and no animation library is required.

05 — Structure

Six tabs is one too many

Cohesion is not only motion. Béa has six tabs and twenty-one routes, which means fifteen screens have no home in the navigation at all. Leading apps in this category run four or five and push the rest into detail planes.

Today · 6 tabs
  • Home — customisable sections
  • World — globe, stats, heatmap
  • Trips — accordion list
  • Recs — the saved vault
  • Near — location-triggered saves
  • You — profile and settings
  • + 15 routes with no tab
Proposed · 5 tabs
  • Home — today, next trip, one nudge
  • Trips — list → trip page
  • Places — the vault, with Near me as a filter
  • World — globe, memories, story, stats
  • You — settings, packing, vault, preferences
  • Every route has a parent

Near is a state, not a place. It answers "what have I saved that is close to me" — which is a filter on the vault, not a separate collection. Merging it frees a tab, ends the duplicate place-card designs, and means proximity can also surface inside Trips, where it is arguably more useful.

06 — Cohesion

Four devices that make twenty-one screens feel like one

Motion makes transitions fluid. These make the destinations feel related — the part that is currently missing and the reason the app reads as a set of pages.

One page header, no exceptions

Eyebrow, serif title, optional trailing action. It already exists informally through the shell — make it a component and forbid a screen from starting any other way. It should compress to a 44px bar on scroll, identically everywhere, so the app has one behaviour instead of twenty-one.

One section primitive

Title, hint, up to two actions, collapsible body. This was built for trips this month (TripSection) and immediately fixed three problems there. Every tab should use it — that alone removes most of the visual drift between screens.

One card anatomy

Media, title, two meta lines, one action strip — in that order, whether it is a trip, a saved place, a city or a memory. Different content, identical skeleton. Recognition is cohesion.

A whisper of tab identity

Each tab gets one hue shift of the shared accent, used in exactly two places: the header rule and the active tab pill. Enough that peripheral vision knows which tab it is; nowhere near enough to fragment the palette. Everything else stays terracotta.

07 — Premium details

The small things people cannot name

  • Optimistic writes everywhere. A tick, a save, a reorder should land before the network does, and roll back visibly if it fails. Partly done; make it the rule.
  • Skeletons shaped like the content. Not spinners, not blank screens — the silhouette of what is coming, so the layout never jumps.
  • Scroll-linked header compression. One behaviour, every screen. It is the cheapest signal that a product was built rather than assembled.
  • Photography carries the surfaces. Trip banners now use the user's own photos; extend the same treatment to city pages and memories. Nothing else raises perceived quality this cheaply.
  • Respect reduced motion. Every transition above drops to a 1ms cross-fade under prefers-reduced-motion. This page does it — the demos above are static if your system asks for that.
  • Haptics where the platform allows. A light tap on save and on undo. Android via the Vibration API; iOS Safari does not expose it, so treat it as an enhancement and never as feedback the design depends on.

08 — Sequencing

Order of work

Deliberately front-loaded: the first two weeks contain the changes a user would notice immediately, so the investment shows before the structural work lands.

Weeks 1–2

Motion foundation

  • Four durations, three curves as tokens; replace all 17 ad-hoc values.
  • Sliding tab indicator and icon weight shift.
  • Sheet plane unified: to-dos, packing, vault, planner all rise identically.
  • Reduced-motion path for everything above.
Weeks 3–5

The detail plane

  • Trips become routes; accordion retired.
  • View Transitions with a shared photo between card and page.
  • Staggered section arrival on every detail screen.
  • Saved places and cities follow the same pattern.
Weeks 6–9

Cohesion

  • Page header, section primitive and card anatomy applied across all tabs.
  • Near folded into Places; navigation down to five tabs.
  • Scroll-linked header, skeletons, optimistic writes.
  • Tab identity hues introduced last, once everything else is consistent.

What this is not

This proposal does not touch Béa's visual identity. The warm palette, the serif, the voice and the photography direction are the product's strongest assets and they stay exactly as they are. Everything above is connective tissue — the grammar between screens, not the look of them.

It also assumes no animation library. The View Transitions API, CSS transitions and about forty lines of token definitions cover every effect specified here. Adding a motion dependency would be the wrong answer to a problem that is architectural.

Prepared as an interface proposal against v8.4.3. Durations, curves and structural counts are measured from the codebase. The demonstrations on this page use the exact values proposed, so what you tapped is what would ship.
