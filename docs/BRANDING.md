# Branding — what Béa is

Internal. Philosophy → brand → UI. Pair with `WHAT_BEA_BELIEVES.md` and `src/lib/bea-voice.ts`.

## Position

**Béa is the place where your travel life lives.**

Not: another AI travel planner · itinerary builder · map · bucket list · trip generator.

## North star

A travel memory, recommendation, and planning companion that helps you remember where
you've been, decide where to go next, and make the most of every opportunity along the way.

## Mission (one line — site, store, deck, onboarding)

**Béa remembers your travel life so Future You doesn't miss what matters.**

## Store copy (source of truth)

**Short:** Save places, remember recommendations, plan trips, and rediscover opportunities wherever you go.

**Medium:** Béa helps you collect your travel life in one place. Save recommendations from friends, track places you've visited, remember cities you want to return to, plan trips from your saved ideas, and rediscover opportunities when you're nearby. Because the best travel plans don't start from scratch. They start with what matters to you.

## Tone rules

- FAQ and help: conversational, not legalistic.
- Privacy/settings: plain English; utilitarian where required; short brand lines on section intros only.
- Never lead with "AI travel planner."
