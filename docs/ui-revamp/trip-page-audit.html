Overview, Now (Companion), Map, Timeline and Bookings, checked against what a traveller needs at each moment, and against the new Home, World and Trips screens. Everything the page does today stays. The proposals change where things sit, what is said once instead of three times, and how it looks.

The flow today

What stands between opening a trip and the answer

Header and navigation

Every tab
  1. Fix firstThe main action is cut off.

    Plan with Béa, To do, Currency and Add stop sit in one row that overflows the screen; "Add stop" is clipped at 414 px wide and disappears on smaller phones. There is no sign the row scrolls.

  2. Fix firstToo much navigation before content.

    Header, pills, tabs, the day row and (on Map) the Live / Split switch stack up to about 350 px. None of it collapses when you scroll.

  3. ImproveNo picture of the trip.

    Home and Trips now show a trip as its stops on the relief map, or a photo. Opening the trip loses that: it becomes a text title. The page should open on the same banner you tapped.

  4. ImproveOne view, three names.

    "Now" (tab), "Companion" (help text and empty state), "Live" (Map switch, which is a different thing). Pick one word per idea.

  5. PolishBéa's line reads as a status, not a voice.

    "Leaving in 5 days and Béa does not know where to yet." sits as small grey text with no avatar. On Home and Trips, Béa speaks in italics beside the dog.

  6. PolishOld palette and type.

    Brown pills, condensed serif and Manrope; the new screens use pink, pastels, Newsreader and Inter with a floating bottom bar.

In the new style

  • Open on the trip banner from Trips: stops illustration or photo (same switch), title on it, countdown or Live badge, travellers' faces.
  • Three round buttons on the banner: Plan with Béa (pink, sparkle), To do (with a count), ⋯ (Add stop, Currency, Settings). Nothing clips.
  • On scroll the banner shrinks to a sticky bar: trip name, day chip, tabs. Tabs become the icon pill row from World.
  • One name: the tab is Today on a travel day and Day 1-style before; the Map's "Live" becomes Follow.

Overview

Before the trip, and to come back to
  1. Fix firstNine tiles, four of them are the tabs again.

    Trip essentials repeats Timeline and Map (already tabs) and Flights, Stays, Transport (the Bookings tab's filters). It reads as a menu, not a summary.

  2. ImproveThree summaries in a row.

    A stats row (2 days · 10 stops · 0 booked), the tile grid and the itinerary list each restate the trip. "No cities …" is truncated in the stats row.

  3. ImproveThe past comes first.

    "Past you in Hiroshima" opens the page, above anything about this trip. It is a lovely card; it belongs lower, next to saved places.

  4. Polish"No city set" as the itinerary's first row.

    An empty state shown as data. The "Find cities" card above already says it.

In the new style

  • Before you go: Home's three cards (To-do, Flight, Packing) with the same pastel colours and progress bar.
  • Days: one card per day with a small stops map (or photo), the first and last stop, and the count. Tapping opens that day on Today, Map or Timeline.
  • Booked: one row of kind chips with counts (Flights 1 · Stays 1 · Transport 3), leading to Bookings.
  • Past you, saved places and "Find cities" move under the days. The Timeline and Map tiles go.

Now (Companion)

On a travel day
  1. Fix first"All" is a dead end here.

    Now follows one day, but the day row offers "All", and before the trip it is selected: the tab opens on "Pick a day to follow." Now should never show "All"; before the trip it opens on Day 1 as a preview.

  2. Fix firstThe leave time is said twice, back to back.

    Béa's bubble says "Leave by 10:44 to reach … at 11:00", and the next block is a big "Leave by 10:44". Béa's line is five lines long because it repeats the stop's full name twice.

  3. ImproveThe day is listed twice.

    Today's progress (a numbered tracker) and Later today (a list) show the same stops one after the other. Tracker labels collide ("Motoyasubashi Pier ferry" runs into its neighbours).

  4. ImproveThe two check-in actions live in different cards.

    Leaving / Not here yet on the current stop, I'm here on the next one. Correct, but neither is a clear primary button.

  5. PolishThe rain notice and "Make the day easier" come last.

    Useful when it rains, but they land after the list where few will scroll.

In the new style

  • Reuse Home's Current stop / Next stop card: one white card, a pulsing pink dot on now, and leave-by as the next stop's first line.
  • One pink primary button that changes with the moment: I'm here → Leaving → I'm here at the next stop. "Not here yet" becomes a text link.
  • The tracker becomes the route ribbon from the Trips banner (dots and short names, "You are here"). Later today folds into it: tap a dot to peek.
  • Béa says one short line ("Leave in 14 min. It's a 16-minute walk."). Rain moves up as a soft pastel chip with "Make the day easier".

Map

Where things are
  1. Fix firstThe whole-trip map is unreadable.

    When stops are close, labels print on top of each other ("Omotesando food crawl / Itsukushima Shrine …"), and the map is the last thing on a very long page.

  2. ImproveSplit is the Timeline again.

    Under each small map comes the full list of stops with walks between them: a third copy of the itinerary. The map itself gets about 330 px.

  3. ImprovePins hide each other and the legend covers them.

    Stops 1, 2 and 7 overlap at the top; the Sights / Food / Transit legend sits on the map's lower right where pins land.

  4. Polish"Live" is not live.

    Live means "map follows one stop at a time". It does not use your location. The word promises tracking.

In the new style

  • Map first: the map fills the screen under the sticky bar; day chips float on it; a bottom sheet holds the stop cards, swiped one by one (today's "Live" layout as the default).
  • Pull the sheet up for the day's list; that replaces Split. The list there is compact: number, time, name.
  • The whole trip uses the relief illustration with city tags and the dashed path, like the Trips header, not a street map.
  • Group pins that touch into one bubble with a count; the legend becomes the kind chips in the sheet.

Timeline

Reading and editing a day
  1. Fix firstEvery stop carries the same two big buttons.

    Map and Directions on all 8 cards: 16 buttons, taller cards, and the stop names lose the eye. They are per-stop shortcuts that fit in the card's ⋯ or a swipe.

  2. Improve"Journey not measured yet" between every stop.

    Seven identical rows, each with a map icon and a chevron. One "Measure the walks" action for the day says it once.

  3. ImproveThree unlabeled round buttons on the day header.

    Signpost, +, ⋯ next to "Day 1 · Wed, Oct 7" push the title onto two lines. The signpost (directions) has no text.

  4. PolishThe gesture hint repeats on every day.

    "Tap a stop to edit · swipe right for done, left to save or delete" in small grey under the first card of each day. Show it once, then never again.

  5. PolishGood details to keep.

    "3 stops within 500 m of each other", the done strike-through, the Current badge and the drag handles all work well.

In the new style

  • Stops as the trip rows from Trips: picture (stops map or photo), serif name, time and stay, kind chip in its pastel, ⋯ on the right.
  • Between stops, a thin dashed line with the walk time in small text, or nothing until measured.
  • Day header: "Day 1" in serif with the date under it, and two labelled pills: + Add and Directions.
  • Editing mode keeps today's power (reorder, retime, swipe actions) behind Edit day.

Bookings

Flights, stays, transport, tickets
  1. ImproveFilters overflow, names truncate.

    "Activities" is cut off; "Arrive Hiroshima …" and "Motoyasubashi P…" lose the part that tells them apart.

  2. ImproveA fifth tab for a list that is often empty.

    Before anything is booked the tab is mostly "not booked yet". It could live in Overview as the Booked strip, opening this full list.

  3. PolishThe "not booked" list is the useful part.

    It is effectively a to-do. Showing it with a "Mark booked" action would make it one.

In the new style

  • Kind filters as World's pastel filter cards (Flights · Stays · Transport · Activities), four across, no overflow.
  • Each booking as a ticket card like Home's "Booked & ready" (route, date, a green tick).
  • "Not booked yet" rows get a Mark booked pill. Names wrap to two lines.
